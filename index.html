Frequently asked questions

How many packs of hair do I need?
It depends on the size and the length. Part 1 has a pack chart by size and length, for kids and for adults, and every style sheet tells you how many packs that style takes. No more buying 3 and running out halfway.
What’s the difference between small, smedium, medium, large and jumbo?
The size guide defines each one with numbers: part size, average number of braids and time. Every parting map also shows the part size row by row, in inches and cm, so “medium” stops being a guess.
How much hair do I add, and when?
The feed-in chapter shows where the first piece goes, how much to use and when to add the second and third, so the root stays flat. Each style’s map has its own feed-in table.
Overhand or underhand? I’m left-handed.
The grip chapter explains both and how to pick one and stick with it, which is what keeps the braids even. Left-handed braiders follow the same steps from the other side.
How do I do the back of my own head?
The map tells you where every section goes before you start, so you work by count, not by sight. The Do It On Yourself bonus (Complete Plan) adds the mirror setup and an order that saves your arms.
How long will it take me?
Every style sheet gives an estimated time. Level 1 styles have bigger parts and fewer braids, so they’re the quickest. With kids, the guide shows how to split a style into shorter sessions.
I’ve never braided before. Is this for me?
Yes. Start with Part 1 and the Level 1 styles: big parts, fewer braids, every number spelled out. Move up to Level 2 and 3 when you’re ready.
Can I do this on short hair or very curly hair? Do I have to straighten it first?
The Preparation part covers washing, drying and stretching the hair before you braid, and every style sheet notes the length it works best on.
What gel, mousse or edge control should I use?
The supplies chapter lists everything you need and what each product is for. No specific brand required: buy what you already trust.
Will it hurt? Will it damage the edges?
Braids pulled too tight at the root put stress on the hairline. The guide shows how to keep the root firm without pulling and which sizes are lighter. It makes no promises about hair health or growth. If something hurts, stop and loosen it.
Is this a video course? How do I get it?
It’s a printable PDF guide with maps, charts and illustrated steps. Right after your payment you get an email with your access. Read it on your phone, tablet or computer, or print it.
Is it a subscription?
No. It’s a one-time payment with lifetime access.
